## Interpretation (honest read)

- **Clears the trade-count floor, but precision is still low (two different criteria).** 105 pooled OOS trades clears the §5.1 100-trade floor, so the result is **not dismissable as under-sampled** — the pre-registration expected ~70–100 and pre-labelled a positive number 'provisional'; we did better on count. *But* §5.7 precision is separate: 22 time-clustered episodes give a **wide** CI, exactly the low precision the pre-registration anticipated. So this **fails to validate** an edge and **cannot confirm** one either — it is not a conclusive proof of *no* edge.
- **PF 1.06 is below the 1.15–1.20 pass bar** — base-cost break-even, not a validated edge, before any robustness penalty.
- **What little is positive rests on one instrument.** Removing the top contributor SGLN drops the pool to **0.90**; dropping the 5 best trades drops it to **0.72** (net $-606); the 90% bootstrap CI **[0.57, 1.49]** includes 1.0. By every frozen robustness check the edge is not distinguishable from none.
- **Only 2 of 4 quarters were net-positive** — W2 carries the pool; the result is not consistent across time (a frozen §5.3 requirement it fails).
- **Cost stress is in the noise band** (1.06 / 1.03 / 1.09) and non-monotonic — at this thin an edge, transaction cost is not the deciding variable; there is no clear signal to stress.
- **Selection is only partly reliable.** Only ['SGLN'] was selected in all 4 windows; the Phase-1b-*dead* name AAPL was selected in 2 of 4. SGLN (selected every window) is the **dominant** contributor and the only *strongly* profitable name — but it is not the only positive one: PLTR/SCCO/SSLN all stayed **weakly** positive OOS (PF ≈ 1.04–1.08). The portfolio reaches only break-even because those gains are marginal and because the selection also pulled in losers (AAPL −$67 dead, ANET −$182 on 4 trades). Remove SGLN and the rest is PF 0.90. So 'select instruments on a training window' did keep the survivors weakly alive, but the only material edge is concentrated in one name — not a portfolio-level effect. On this evidence, training-window selection is **not a reliable standalone procedure**, independent of the PF.
- **Net:** the apparent Phase 1b edge does **not** generalize out-of-sample as a portfolio — the survivors mostly stayed *just* above water and the only real contribution is SGLN. The only thing keeping the frozen tree out of the 'clearly negative → STOP' branch is SGLN holding the pool above 1.0; the weight of evidence sits at the negative end of flat. Extending history is the registered lever for the thin names, but it is **unlikely** to manufacture a portfolio edge given three flat OOS quarters and a single-name positive — and the wide upper CI does not license a hard forward claim either way.
